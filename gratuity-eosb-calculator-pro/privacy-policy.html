Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Overview

Gratuity & EOSB Calculator Pro helps you estimate end-of-service benefits (gratuity, indemnity, separation or retirement pay, depending on the country you select) based on the employment details you enter. Every calculation runs entirely on your device — this app has no server backend, so there is nowhere for your inputs to be sent even if we wanted to.

Information we do not collect

Your salary, employment dates, exit reason, and every other calculator input are processed only in the app's memory while you use it. We never transmit them to us or to any analytics provider. The app does not require an account, a sign-in, or any personal identifier to be used.

No advertising

This is the paid version of Gratuity & EOSB Calculator. It shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK activity, and does not use your Advertising ID or any other identifier for ad purposes. If you're looking for the free, ad-supported version of this app, it's a separate Play Store listing with its own privacy policy.

Analytics

We may collect non-identifying usage analytics — for example, which screen was opened or that a calculation was started — to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. These events never include your salary, dates, or any other calculation input.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention

Because calculations are processed only on-device and there is no server component, we do not retain your calculation data at all. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored locally.

Your choices

You can uninstall the app at any time to remove all local data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@example.com.
